DASFAA 2027 Teen Science and Technology Innovation Forum

Call for Papers

Springer Publication · To Be Submitted for EI Compendex Indexing

Introduction

The DASFAA 2027 Teen Science and Technology Innovation Forum (青少年科创论坛) invites high school students to share their research in database systems, data management, data science, and artificial intelligence. The forum offers students an opportunity to present their work, receive feedback from researchers, and participate in the international DASFAA community.

We welcome original ideas, exploratory studies, early-stage methods, and innovative applications. Submissions should address a clear research question and support their conclusions with appropriate reasoning or experimental evidence. Reviewers will assess the work with expectations appropriate for high school students.

Topics of Interest

Topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Database systems, query processing, indexing, and storage
  • Data collection, integration, cleaning, and quality
  • Graph, vector, spatial, temporal, streaming, and multimedia data
  • Data mining, knowledge discovery, and machine learning
  • Large language models and retrieval-augmented generation
  • Knowledge graphs, information retrieval, and recommendation
  • Data visualization and interactive data analysis
  • Data privacy, security, fairness, and responsible AI
  • Data-driven applications in education, healthcare, environmental science, smart cities, and other domains

Eligibility

  • The first author must be enrolled in high school or an equivalent upper-secondary education program at the time of submission.
  • High school students must be the primary contributors to the research.
  • Teachers, mentors, and researchers may serve as co-authors, provided that their contributions are acknowledged accurately.
  • At least one eligible student author must present accepted work at the forum.

Important Dates

  • Paper submission deadline: February 15, 2027
  • Acceptance notification: March 15, 2027
  • Camera-ready deadline: March 30, 2027
  • Forum date: During DASFAA 2027. The exact date will be announced.

Submission and camera-ready deadlines are at 23:59 Anywhere on Earth (AoE).

Submission Guidelines

  • Papers must be written in English and submitted as PDF files.
  • Papers must follow the Springer Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) single-column format, as used by the DASFAA 2027 main research track.
  • Papers must not exceed eight pages in total, including figures, tables, references, and appendices.
  • Each paper should include an abstract of no more than 200 words, following the main track’s guidelines.
  • Submissions must report original work that has not been published in a refereed conference or journal and is not under review elsewhere.
  • Authors must accurately describe student and mentor contributions, acknowledge data and software sources, and disclose relevant uses of generative AI.

Review Process

The Track Chairs will coordinate peer review by researchers in relevant areas. Reviewers will consider the research question, originality, technical soundness, supporting evidence, clarity, and student contribution.

The Track Chairs may request clarification of student contributions or a brief discussion with student authors. Authors must declare relevant conflicts of interest in the submission system.

Accepted papers must incorporate requested revisions and comply with the formatting requirements by the camera-ready deadline.

Publication and Indexing

Accepted papers will be published by Springer and submitted for EI Compendex indexing. Final inclusion in EI Compendex is subject to the indexing service’s evaluation.

Authors must submit the revised camera-ready paper by March 30, 2027 and comply with the publisher’s formatting and publication requirements.
