Call for Tutorial Proposals

Important Dates

All deadlines are 23:59 Anywhere on Earth (AoE) time.

  • Proposal Submission Deadline: January 15th, 2027
  • Notification Date: January 25th, 2027

DASFAA 2027 will continue the conference tradition of providing conference attendees with high-quality tutorials on the state-of-the-art topics in research, development, and applications in database technologies and advanced data management, processing, and analysis systems. We invite tutorial proposals from distinguished researchers, engineers, and practitioners on emerging topics of significant interest to the DASFAA community. We note that a good tutorial should not focus solely on the presenters' previous work, but represent a complete review of all significant work in the area of the topic. Each tutorial will have an assigned length of 1.5 hours (short) or 3 hours (long). Long tutorials may be offered a short tutorial slot upon acceptance depending on conference logistics.

Submission Guidelines

Tutorial proposals must be submitted electronically in PDF format (maximum 6 pages, LNCS style). Each proposal should include the following information:

  • Tutorial Title
  • Presenter Contact Information: Names, affiliations and email addresses. Include a short biography for each presenter
  • Tutorial Length: Short (1.5 hours) or Long (3 hours)
  • Motivation: Explain the relevance and importance of the tutorial topic and particularly why it is of interest to the DASFAA community
  • Outline: Provide a brief outline of the tutorial content
  • Presentation History: Indicate whether the tutorial has been presented elsewhere; if so, specify when and where. Please also note similar tutorials in related database conferences, noting where this tutorial differs from existing tutorials.
  • Key References: List significant references related to the tutorial

Submitted tutorial proposals will be reviewed by the organizers based on the significance of the topic, expertise of the presenters and the balance between breadth and depth of content. Accepted tutorials will be scheduled as parallel sessions during the main DASFAA 2027 conference, and tutorial slides will be made available on the conference website.

An honorarium will be provided for each accepted tutorial (note: tutorial speakers are still required to register for the conference). All presenters listed on the tutorial are expected to attend DASFAA 2027 in person.

Proposals should be sent to dasfaa2027tutorials@gmail.com
