Call for PhD Forum

The DASFAA PhD Forum provides a platform for PhD students to present an early-stage research project and to receive comments and suggestions from the PhD Forum Committee, which consists of leading scientists and experts in DASFAA related areas. Applicants should be PhD students who have identified a research direction but have not yet formalised their thesis proposal. Submissions should present a research proof of concept: a clearly stated research problem, the proposed approach, and an initial prototype or preliminary results that demonstrate its feasibility, together with a plan for how the work will develop.

All submissions will be reviewed by the committee. Accepted submissions will be included in the conference proceedings, and the corresponding authors will be invited to give a short lightning talk at DASFAA 2027, followed by a poster session where they can interact directly with committee members and attendees. Participants will receive constructive feedback on their project and research trajectory from established experts and build lasting collaborative relationships with a global cohort of peers.

  • Paper submission deadline: January 5, 2027

  • Camera-ready due: TBA

Submission Guidelines

Interested PhD students should submit a proposal describing their proposed research and progress. Submissions must not exceed 4 pages, in LNCS (Lecture Notes in Computer Science) format, including references. The proposal should follow the same outline, details, and format as regular DASFAA papers. A proposal must be written in English and include:

  • The title of the submission and the author’s name
  • The research problem and motivation, with a brief positioning against related work
  • The proposed approach and its expected significance
  • The proof of concept: an initial prototype or preliminary results demonstrating the feasibility of the approach
  • A research plan outlining key milestones, the planned evaluation, and open questions on which the author would most value feedback

The submission should be submitted as a PDF file.

Review Process

The submitted PhD proposals will be evaluated based on several factors:

  • The novelty and relevance of the research problem
  • A clear understanding of current developments in the related areas
  • The soundness and significance of the proposed approach
  • The feasibility of the approach as demonstrated by the proof of concept
  • The clarity of the research plan and the potential contribution of the work

All accepted submissions must be presented at the conference as both a lightning talk and a poster session. Details on the presentation format will be shared with authors upon acceptance.
